# Mythos-Ban Demo Plan

Output of the Katechon demo director prompt
(`docs/demo-episode-director-prompt.md`) run against the Fable 5 / Mythos 5 ban
source packet. This plan is a content draft only. The correct implementation
must reuse the Build With Us format while preserving the future-demo voice rule:
exactly two voices, Narrator and Kat, matching the fast-image-gen interaction
pattern.

> **Implementation correction:** do not ship this as one continuous Kat VO.
> Build With Us supplies the bounded stage, Kat placement, matrix transition,
> and brand cards. The episode still needs a two-voice audio layer: Narrator
> asks and frames; Kat answers through the in-app voice path. If the current
> renderer cannot yet mix both voices, that is pipeline work, not a reason to
> weaken the format requirement.

## 1. Demo Thesis
Katechon turns a breaking, source-cited event — the US export-control order that
forced Anthropic to pull Fable 5 and Mythos 5 — into a live, interactive channel
that rebuilds itself on demand, generating the explanatory state (timeline,
model status, the jailbreak schematic, the market verdict) in front of you.

## 3. Two-Voice Script

**Narrator:** "Anthropic just got ordered to pull two models. Kat, build me the
channel around the Fable 5 ban."

**Kat:** "On it. I am binding the directive timestamp, affected models, and the
market rail into one generated state."

**Narrator:** "Good. Now show the thing they are actually scared of, and whether
the market thinks access comes back."

**Kat:** "Generating that angle now: the jailbreak is a codebase-read and
fix-flaws path. I am rendering it as a schematic, then pinning the market
verdict beside it: restored by Monday, three percent."

**Narrator:** "That is the loop. Source in, question asked, channel rebuilt,
and the answer becomes a video state."

## Render result (2026-06-16)

Built and rendered. Implementation:
- Renderer `public/dashboards/identities/mythos-ban.{js,css}` (Build With Us
  engine, anime/Anthropic palette `mythosBan`). Registered in `catalog.js`,
  `server.js`, and the deck wiring in `public/index.html`.
- Two-voice audio is real: `/api/speak` now takes a `voice` param; Narrator =
  ElevenLabs George (`NARRATOR_VOICE_ID`), Kat = the pitch voice. The renderer
  pre-bakes all six lines in parallel and sequences them (Kat in-frame audio +
  avatar mime; Narrator in-frame VO).
- Media: `scripts/generate-mythos-ban-visuals.js` (Seedance videos + Seedream
  schematic), assets in `public/videos/mythos-ban/` and
  `public/generated/mythos-ban/`.
- Composite capture stage: `public/capture/mythos-ban-stage.html` overlays the
  **actual Kat Live2D avatar** (`avatar-pet.html`, transparent, lip-syncing the
  Kat lines via its `{type:'audio'}` message) and a matrix rebuild veil
  (fired on each `seed-channel-build-transition`) over the reel — without the
  deck's remote-desktop dependency. The reel is embed-aware: Kat lines route to
  the host avatar, Narrator stays an in-frame VO.
- Capture: `scripts/capture-mythos-ban.sh` (Xvfb + PulseAudio null sink + kiosk
  Chromium + ffmpeg x11grab). Output: **`artifacts/mythos-ban/mythos-ban-demo.mp4`**
  (36s, 1280×720, H.264+AAC, **two-voice audio + the Kat avatar composited in**)
  + `mythos-ban-poster.jpg`.

Capture gotchas (encoded in the script): software GL (`--use-angle=swiftshader
--enable-unsafe-swiftshader`) is required for the Live2D/PIXI avatar and the
WebGL globe; accelerated video decode must be disabled
(`--disable-accelerated-video-decode --disable-gpu-memory-buffer-video-frames`)
or the GPU process crashes when software GL meets 1080p video; and Chromium must
be forced to X11 (`--ozone-platform=x11`, `WAYLAND_DISPLAY` unset) so it renders
into the offscreen Xvfb rather than the host Wayland session.

The native deck path (Kat in the deck's own corner slot) is also wired in
`index.html` for `mythos-ban`, but the local `/deck/` boot drops into the
remote-desktop fallback (no HLS stream locally); full deck capture uses the
canonical `make record-start` pipeline on the stream host.
